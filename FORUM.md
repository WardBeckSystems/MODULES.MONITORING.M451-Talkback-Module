> I'm a big fan of the M451 I have lots of them and lots of plans for them
>
> The M451 has 4 buttons, they can latch or not latch. So when you push a button you can hold it in or it's a temporary hold. This can be done by removing a little metal piece. The M451 has 2 inputs and 4 outputs. For every button you push a different voltage goes out the pressed ctrl out. The voltage is 24 V. How it can be used is to mute something, push a relay, turn a logic latch a certain way etc etc. the modules on your console that are controlled are the M495 Stereo Monitor Module that has a talkback input (pins 11 and M) when the signal is sent into the module it talks over the assigned input. 
>
> so I want to talk to:
> Studio 1 - put it into the studio M495 monitor input and take that input and put it into the monitors then take the voltage from this and feed it into the headphone mute voltage ctrl. when you push the studio button you can mute the headphones in studio 1. Or have a light come on whatever you want. 
> Studio 2 - put it into the studio M495 monitor input and take that input and put it into the monitors
> Headphone 1 - put it into the studio M495 monitor input and take that input and put it into the headphones
> Headphone 2 - put it into the studio M495 monitor input and take that input and put it into the headphones
>
> you can do whatever you want it to do by sending the right signal and right voltage where you want it.
>
> What I would assign the headphones is a mix made from P2. Since your M490modules have P(rogram)1 and P(rogram)2 P1 goes to air while P2 can be your internal monitor system or a mix minus. (or use the aux send to create a headphone mix) Say you have music beds underneath what you're talking over by creating a mix minus for the headphones you dont hear the music
>
> I know this is a general and broad... but tell me more what you want it to do, and more about what it's doing now. check the manual... if ou have one. If you dont have one... be prepared to rip it all apart to do what you want it to, or prepared to live with what it's doing
